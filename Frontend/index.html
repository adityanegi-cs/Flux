Frequently Asked Questions

Is my financial data safe?

Absolutely. Security is our top priority. In this specific version of Flux, we utilize a "Local-First" architecture. This means all your financial data—transactions, budget limits, and savings history—is stored entirely on your own device's browser storage (LocalStorage). We do not transmit, store, or sell your private financial data to any external servers or third parties. You have complete control over your data. If you clear your browser cache, your data is wiped.

How do I earn points?

Earning points on Flux is designed to be engaging and rewarding. You can accumulate points through several methods: 1) Consistency: Logging your expenses daily grants a streak bonus. 2) Savings: For every rupee you save (Income minus Expenses), you earn points. 3) Learning: Completing financial literacy modules in our Academy section rewards you with Points. 4) Challenges: Participating in weekly challenges like "No Spend Days" offers massive point boosts.

Are the rewards real?

In a fully deployed production environment, yes! Flux partners with top student-friendly brands like Spotify, Netflix, Amazon, and Zomato to provide real, redeemable gift codes. In this demonstration version, we simulate the entire redemption process to show you exactly how the gamification loop incentivizes better financial habits.
